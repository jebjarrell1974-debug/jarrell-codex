## Why MPL-2.0 and not GPL

MPL-2.0 is file-level copyleft. Anyone who modifies these files must publish
those modifications, but they may combine this code with differently licensed
code without relicensing their whole application.

That second property matters here for one concrete reason. The GPL forbids
imposing further restrictions on users, and Apple's App Store terms impose
exactly such restrictions. The two are widely held to be incompatible — VLC
was removed from the App Store in 2011 over this. Choosing GPL would have made
an iOS release legally untenable. MPL-2.0 keeps the code open while leaving
every distribution channel available: Play Store, App Store, and a sideloaded
APK handed to someone directly.
